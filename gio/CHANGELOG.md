## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-31

### Added - First playable lava run / Primera corrida de lava 🌋

Gio's words: *"volcanoes and a bunch of lava and you gotta try to get through the lava and
there's cracks you've been falling thru."*

- Lava world with volcanoes 🌋 in the background
- Rock platforms with **cracks** you fall through
- Lava pools that burn you
- 3 lives ❤️, respawn at the start when the lava or a crack gets you
- Finish flag 🏁 on the far side of the lava
